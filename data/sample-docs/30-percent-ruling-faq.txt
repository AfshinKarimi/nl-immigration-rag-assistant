30% Ruling — Frequently Asked Questions (Sample Demo Document)

What is the 30% ruling?
The 30% ruling is a tax facility for employees who are recruited or seconded
from abroad to work in the Netherlands and who possess specific expertise
that is scarce in the Dutch labour market. Under the ruling, an employer may
grant a tax-free allowance of up to 30% of the gross salary, subject to a
salary threshold, without the employee having to submit evidence of
actual extraterritorial costs.

Who is eligible?
An employee is generally eligible if they were recruited from outside the
Netherlands, lived more than 150 kilometers from the Dutch border for more
than 16 of the 24 months prior to starting employment, and meet the minimum
taxable salary threshold set annually by the Dutch tax authority.

How long does the ruling last?
As of the most recent reform, the maximum duration of the 30% ruling is
five years from the start date of employment, provided all conditions
continue to be met throughout that period.

How do I apply?
The employer and employee jointly submit an application to the Dutch Tax
and Customs Administration (Belastingdienst). This is a sample/demo
document only — always verify current thresholds and rules on the official
Belastingdienst or IND website before relying on this information.
